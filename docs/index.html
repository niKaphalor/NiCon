    One console for every game server you run.

    NiCon speaks RCON to Rust, Palworld, ARK, Arma 3, DayZ, Garry's Mod, and more — sign in to manage your servers from any browser.

    • Every game, one consoleA single browser tab for RCON across Rust, Palworld, ARK, Arma 3, DayZ, Garry's Mod, and more — no extra software to install.
    • Kick and ban in a clickLive player lists parsed straight out of RCON output, with one-click kick and ban instead of typing raw commands.
    • Import from NitradoPaste your API token once and NiCon fills in host, port, and protocol for every eligible server automatically.
    • Encrypted at restRCON passwords and Nitrado tokens are stored with AES-256-GCM and never sent back to your browser once saved.
    • Your relay, your infrastructureThe piece that actually talks RCON is self-hosted — run it locally or on your own server, never ours.

      Settings

      Account

      Delete account

      Permanently deletes your account and every server you've added — RCON passwords included. This can't be undone.

      Privacy

      Your server list and RCON passwords are stored for your account only — no one else can see, edit, or connect through them. See the privacy policy for details.

      Cloud API

      Handles sign-in, your account, and your server list — a normal web address, reachable whether or not your computer is on.

      Relay

      NiCon needs a relay — a small bridge process — to actually speak RCON to a server: browsers can't open raw TCP or WebRCON sockets on their own. Get it here.

      Admin

      Every account on this NiCon instance. Regenerating a code or deleting an account takes effect immediately — hand any regenerated code to that person yourself, there's no email to send it to.
